# Review 2026-09-30: after phases 1–6

Four independent read-only reviews, the same split as the 2026-09-29
baseline:
- order safety (the bridge and the engine fork);
- the macOS app and packaging;
- experience and design;
- product, completeness and docs.

**Verdict:** the confirm gate held. No reviewer found a path to an order
without `y` on the card that is on screen, and a 30-run send-vs-price
stress test sent nothing unseen. The gaps are at the edges:
- outcomes can be hidden or lost around overlays, shutdown and resting
  orders;
- the alarm doesn't name the order;
- the packaged app can't run on another Mac;
- the docs have drifted from the code.

## Findings, grouped into work

### A. Truth and safety

**A1. Outcomes hide behind overlays and are still acknowledged. HIGH, confirmed.**
- Where: `Model.swift:136-146, 260-266`.
- Pane, setup, login, command, calibrate and question all outrank the strip.
- `y` with the Positions pane open means "sending" and a rejection are never
  seen, yet `/ack` tells the engine they were.
- Fix: the order flow and outcomes outrank every overlay, and the face acks
  only what is on screen.

**A2. Shutdown can open, send and abandon an order. MEDIUM, confirmed.**
- Where: `bridge.py` `_confirm`/`decide` never check `_closing`.
- `SHUTDOWN_WAIT` is 30 s, below the ~90 s worst case after a send.
- On relaunch the app `/shutdown`s a leftover bridge that still holds an
  unacknowledged outcome (`BridgeProcess.swift:107-125`).
- The quit deadline gives up silently (`AppDelegate.swift:74-81`).
- Fix:
  - refuse cards and sends while closing;
  - wait for the sent command's result, up to 120 s;
  - `/shutdown` returns 409 while an outcome is held;
  - the quit deadline raises the alarm instead of quitting.

**A3. Resting orders aren't durable. MEDIUM, confirmed.**
- `fill` and `working` events are neither replayed nor logged.
- One sticky slot: a new `inflight` can overwrite an unacknowledged outcome.
- A watcher that dies or times out drops "1 resting" silently.
- A watcher fill overwrites a newer order's "sending" or "Rejected"
  (`Model.swift:234`).
- Fix:
  - the bus keeps the working set and the unacknowledged outcomes and fills,
    replayed on subscribe;
  - `face_log("fill")`;
  - an explicit "no longer watched" outcome;
  - fills get their own line;
  - the working set comes from engine status and resets when the engine
    restarts.

**A4. The exit readback lost the expiry. LOW–MEDIUM, confirmed.**
- `_held_when` uses the index cadence. A 6 Oct contract is read back as
  "weekly".
- The exit card headline is "EXIT", not the side.
- P&L is at LTP, not at the sending price (+₹26 shown vs about +₹3).
- `lots` is None.
- Fix:
  - say the date unless it is the nearest expiry;
  - headline "SELL 23150 CALL" with the closing banner;
  - P&L at `preview["price"]` and `lots` sent, in the fork, with tests.

**A5. A refusal after a question stays hidden. MEDIUM, confirmed.** The stale
question stays and `1`–`3` stay captured (`Model.swift:296`). Fix: always
clear the question on a non-question result.

**A6. Gate hardening. LOW.**
- `apply_price` bounds before rounding: `-1e308` gives a 500, and the echo
  is unbounded.
- Reset `_card_shown` on every card emit.
- Random start for `card_id`, or `run` in `/confirm`.
- Honour a recorded decision over "timeout".
- Refuse `/login` while a card or order is live, and expire `_login`.
- Require a custom header, so a web page can't become a card "viewer".

**A7. Tests touch live state and miss the real agent.**
- The engine tests rewrite the live `sayso/.daily_limits.json`
  (`test_imports.py:85`). Point `SAYSO_HOME` at a temp folder in tests.
- `config.save` is not atomic.
- Add:
  - one integration test driving the real `voice.agent` and `watch` through
    `Engine` and HTTP with a stubbed broker;
  - tests for the races above;
  - Swift tests for overlay priority.

### B. Experience and design

**B1. The alarm doesn't say which order.** The `inflight` event already
carries it. Show "BUY 65 · Nifty 29 Sep 23150 CALL · ≈72.70" and the
matching order-book row. Say it once, not twice. Allow the Orders pane
while the alarm is up.

**B2. Orange and amber clash, and amber means a dozen things.**
- Sell `#FF9E1C` vs warn `#FFC733` is ΔE 9 under deuteranopia.
- Re-cut:
  - sell `#FF8A00`;
  - warn `#FFE45C`, only for "look twice at this order";
  - red only for danger (not for rejections or negative P&L);
  - white for primary buttons.

**B3. The glance.**
- Show the side as a solid tile, with a 4 pt side-colour edge.
- Index and expiry at 16 pt white; the total at 17 pt dim.
- A `y` keycap that fills when the 0.7 s dwell has passed, replacing
  "Too quick".

**B4. The contract stays through the whole loop.** "SENDING · BUY 65 ·
Nifty … CALL", then "Bought 65 @ 72.35" or "Rejected · …" for every
outcome, not only fills.

**B5. Voice.** Zoe pauses about 0.4 s at every comma.
- The option readback measures 3.5 s, not 2.9.
- Cut it to "Buy 1 lot Nifty 23 1 50 call." (2.7 s): no commas, and the
  expiry only when it isn't the default.
- Shorter resting, unknown and question lines.
- A/B test Tara (en_IN, installed).

**B6. Marked lines from the spec.**
- "Strike adjusted" is only in `spoken`. Add `strike_adjusted_from` to the
  preview, show it, count it as unusual and say it.
- Do the same for "at-the-money chosen".

**B7. Copy.**
- "Nothing was sent." under every refusal.
- No roadmap words, and no raw exchange codes.
- ₹ with Indian grouping in the engine's messages.
- Friendly startup and error lines.
- A LIVE badge on the disconnected shape.
- Fix the mic intro truncation.
- Size panes to their content.
- Collapse to five type sizes.
- The engine sends the unusual reasons; the ₹25,000 threshold is currently
  hard-coded in the face.
