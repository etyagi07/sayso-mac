# Review 2026-09-29: the baseline, and the plan to raise it

> **Superseded by `docs/review-2026-09-30.md`** (independent re-review,
> about 6/10, and waves A–C since). The Progress section below records
> phases 1–6 as they were done, and its self-assessed scores were optimistic.

Four read-only reviews (design, experience, engineering, product) of the Sayso
engine inside the ProjectZero body kit. **Verdict: the engine is race-ready and
the confirm gate is top-grade; the body is a road-legal prototype, not a
showroom car.**

| 2026-09-29 | Phase 6, below | Packaging 7: built and run on 2026-09-30 (unsigned) · Voice 6 with Zoe (Premium) |

Phase 6 as built:
- **No `#filePath`.** A dev build (`build.sh`) names its checkout in
  Info.plist. A packaged build (`package.sh`) carries the engine, and on
  first launch and each update it installs it into
  `~/Library/Application Support/Sayso`. The install is version-stamped,
  staged and moved in whole, and tested.
- **Per-user state.** The fork's `SAYSO_HOME` moves sessions, account
  settings, limits, `config.json` and symbol masters together, and the
  bridge's logs follow. Unset, state stays beside the code as before (this
  Mac's live login is untouched).
- **The speech model is pinned** to revision `52a88bf…` and loads from the
  local cache, with no network, when present.
- **Logs rotate** at 5 MB (`face*.jsonl`, `bridge.log`), keeping one older
  file.
- **A torch-free dependency set** (`app/requirements-dist.txt`), about
  650 MB smaller. `package.sh` checks the packaged engine imports, loads the
  model and never pulls in torch.
- **Blocked on a download:** a relocatable CPython 3.13 (python-build-standalone)
  for `PYTHON_RUNTIME`. This Mac has only 3.11 standalone and the Homebrew
  3.13 behind the venv.
- **Later:** Developer ID signing and notarisation, including the embedded
  Python. Numba and MLX will need the JIT entitlements under the hardened
  runtime.

Phase 1 as built:
- The outcome of a sent order is held by the engine and replayed to every
  reconnect until the face acknowledges it (`POST /ack`).
- If the face loses the engine with an order out, it raises "ORDER MAY BE
  LIVE". Quit waits up to 30 s for the result, and so does the engine on
  SIGTERM.
- Demo runs on 8788, live on 8787, and one panel at a time. `/shutdown` is
  refused while any window is using the engine.
- A closed face connection is noticed in 0.25 s and cancels its card. A card
  nobody can see is cancelled at once (`no_face`).
- Cards expire on the wall clock too, and the app cancels them on sleep.
- The port is bound before the engine loads. Exit 3 means the port is taken
  and exit 4 means the engine failed; both are shown on the panel. Restarts
  back off to 60 s, and `bridge.log` is appended, not truncated.
- `face.jsonl` lines carry mode, card_id, symbol, quantity, price, tag and
  order_no.
- A price must be above zero after tick rounding; absurd values are refused.
  Every handler has a top-level guard.
- A refused command says so. `_confirm` never leaves a card open. Session
  and IP status refresh every 2 minutes.
- The demo engine now runs the real `Engine` code with scripted modules
  underneath.

Still open from the engineering review: the OAuth `state` check (small), and
Robustness items that belong to Phase 6 (`#filePath`, log rotation).

## Decisions (2026-09-29, later)

- **Product for many Mac traders.** The first client is happy with Sayso v1.
  This is about getting ready for a larger pool. macOS only.
- **The engine may change**, in the fork (`sayso/`) only, with tests. The
  client's copy is never touched.
- **Order of work:** safety and truth first, then tests.
- **Distribution:** a self-contained `Sayso.app` that installs its engine into
  `~/Library/Application Support`. Signed and notarised later, once there is a
  Developer ID.
- **Launch:** always opens live.

## Phase 1 — truth after `y`, robustness (engineering review F1–F9)

- **F1** A sent order's result can vanish on a stream drop, quit, crash or
  `/shutdown`.
  - Fix: keep the in-flight outcome and replay it to new subscribers until
    acknowledged.
  - Fix: the face raises "may be live" if it loses the engine while sending.
  - Fix: shutdown and quit wait while an order is out.
- **F2** A demo app beside the live app shares port 8787. The two watchdogs
  kill each other's engine, and the "demo" can drive the live one.
  - Fix: demo gets its own port.
  - Fix: allow one instance only.
  - Fix: only ever shut down an engine this app started.
  - Fix: refuse commands when the engine's mode differs from the app's.
- **F3** A dropped connection doesn't cancel the card until the timeout.
  - Fix: cancel when the subscriber that received the card goes away.
  - Fix: a faster keepalive.
- **F4** Sleep freezes the card deadline (`time.monotonic` doesn't advance
  during sleep).
  - Fix: check wall-clock time too.
  - Fix: the app cancels the card on will-sleep.
- **F5** Crash or port-taken loops are invisible, and `bridge.log` is truncated
  on every start.
  - Fix: bind the port before loading the engine.
  - Fix: back off between restarts, append to the log, and say why on screen.
- **F6** `face.jsonl` can't be matched to orders.
  - Fix: add mode, card_id, symbol, qty, price and tag.
- **F7** Price edge cases.
  - Fix: check "> 0" after tick rounding.
  - Fix: guard against overflow.
  - Fix: a top-level guard in `do_POST`.
- **F8/F9** Silent 409s, a zombie card if `_confirm` throws, stale session
  status.
  - Fix: show feedback for 409s.
  - Fix: `try/finally` in `_confirm`.
  - Fix: periodic health checks.

## Phase 2 — tests

- Confirm-step tests over HTTP against the fake engine: dwell, card_id, price
  vs send, disconnect, timeout, shutdown, outcome replay.
- Exception after send → "unknown".
- `apply_price` parity with `cli._set_price`.
- A contract test on the engine names the bridge relies on.
- Swift `Model` sequence tests.

## Phase 3 — voice and speed

- An Enhanced en-IN voice, with the rate kept at ~190–200. **Not 170: slower
  makes it longer.**
- `silence_seconds` ~0.9.
- Short trader-idiom readbacks (engine), under 2 s. Currently numbers take
  3.5 s of a 5.7 s readback.
- Short result lines.
- Show what was heard after every refusal.
- An earcon when the mic opens, a level meter, a speaking indicator.
- No replaying stale lines, and one alarm sound.

## Phase 4 — look and feel

- One meaning per colour: take red back from LIVE.
- Strike and CE/PE glanceable.
- The spec's footprint, with no dead bands.
- Unusual orders change proportion.
- Structured results.
- An alarm card that looks wrong from across the room.
- A type scale with nothing under 11 pt.
- A translucent surface.
- A wordmark or icon.
- A sensible disconnected state.

## Phase 5 — completeness

- Funds, positions and orders panes (a regression from the terminal).
- Health checks and a first-run wizard.
- Session and IP problems as visible states.
- A resting order kept visible.
- Keys for quick-picks.
- Remembered client and user IDs (never the secret).

## Phase 6 — packaging

- Remove `#filePath`.
- Bundle the bridge, the engine and a Python runtime into the .app, and
  install them into Application Support on first launch.
- Engine state goes to per-user paths.
- Pin the Whisper model.
- Rotate logs.
- Later: Developer ID signing and notarisation.
